Can I use the Contracts folder templates & rules?YES — it's the backbone

The walkthrough — button to signed & filed

Scenario: rep is on the CG-Roofing-style lead in the CRM.

1

Rep presses "Generate Agreement" on the lead

Rep · in the leads CRM (dashboard)

Button lives on the lead/account page. First choice is the template picker — preloaded registry, starting with Phase 1 Services Agreement (your engine). New templates get added to the registry once, by us, and every rep sees them.

2

The pre-send check — a required popup; nothing sends blind

Rep · every field shows its pre-filled guess and must be CONFIRMED

The CRM often won't have perfect data at signing time — that's expected. This modal exists precisely for that: pre-fills are suggestions to confirm or correct, and the send button stays disabled until every required field is confirmed.

Popup: Pre-send check (Agreement Builder)
  • Legal entity name — CONFIRM/EDIT — separate from brand: legal name goes in the agreement preamble (e.g. "CG Roofing and Waterproofing LLC and Red Rock Roofing LLC")
  • DBA / brand name — CONFIRM/EDIT — what they're known as (e.g. "CG Roofing & Red Rock Roofing"); prints on the d/b/a line and cover email
  • Business office / address — CONFIRM/EDIT — flagged "unverified" until a human confirms it once
  • Entity descriptor — CONFIRM/EDIT ("each a Florida limited liability company")
  • Scope — PICKED, NOT TYPED: standard package (1 entity / 1 website / 1 Second Brain + social) preselected; entity editor for extras; any deviation goes in a "non-standard adjustments" box that routes to Rob for approval — same rule as the CG split-payment approval PDF
  • Fee — COMPUTED from package + entities; editable only with approval
  • Authorized signer — CONFIRM/OVERRIDE — defaults to the lead's KDM, but fully overridable: name + email + mobile number (send to whoever actually signs — an office manager, a partner, counsel)
  • Delivery channel — PICK: Email · SMS · Both
  • MLE representative — AUTO = the logged-in rep, or Rob if the rep lacks signing authority (field-visibility rule from the CRM PRD)

Under the hood this popup is your client JSON — same schema as prospects/cg_roofing.json, with legal-vs-DBA now first-class. The intake gate carries over: engine hard-refuses to generate unless the confirmed intake block matches the entities list. Bonus: every confirmed value writes back to the lead record — the CRM gets cleaner with every agreement sent.

3

Engine generates the PDF

System · your existing engine, wrapped as an API

The form saves the JSON → calls phase1_engine.py (wrapped in a small POST /generate API, exactly as your onboarding PRD already planned) → out comes the same agreement PDF you sent CG Roofing, with the §5/§6 as-delivered wording. A row is appended to agreement-ledger.csv (version, generated date, status=draft) and the JSON lands in prospects/<slug>.json.

4

Preview + approval gate

Rep previews · Rob approves deviations only

Rep sees the rendered PDF in-browser (react-pdf). Standard-scope agreements go straight to send. Non-standard ones show "Awaiting Rob's approval" — Rob gets the ping, taps approve, and the approval is logged like today's approval PDFs.

5

Customer signs on a link — legally binding

Authorized signer · any device, no account
Screen: Signing page (their side)
  • Email and/or SMS with a single-use, expiring link → opens the agreement for full review. SMS is just as binding — ESIGN doesn't regulate the delivery channel, and phone possession ties identity at least as tightly as email. SMS via Twilio (~$0.008/text + one-time A2P 10DLC business-texting registration)
  • Consent checkbox — ESIGN/UETA electronic-business consent (consumer version with §7001(c) disclosures if the signer is a homeowner)
  • Signature — draw with finger/mouse (signature_pad) or type name rendered in script; printed name field beside it
  • Date — AUTO-STAMPED server-side at the moment of signing (never self-reported)
  • Every event logged: sent (channel + destination), viewed, consented, signed — with timestamp, IP, device
  • Copy retention (legal requirement): completion screen always offers a download button + "email me my copy" field — so SMS-only signers can still keep their copy

MLE side: the MLE representative (Rob, or an authorized rep) countersigns the same way from inside the CRM — same signature box, same auto-date. Both signatures + printed names + dates are stamped onto the signature block of the PDF by the server.

6

Completion — stamped, certified, filed, advanced

System · zero manual steps

Server re-verifies the document hash (proves nothing changed since send), stamps both signatures, appends an audit-certificate page (every event, hash, parties — the page that wins the court fight), emails final copies to the signer and MLE, and files the PDF into the Contracts project as agreements/Phase 1 Agreement - <Client> v<N> (complete).pdf — your organize.py naming — with the ledger row flipped to signed and superseded versions moved to archive/.

CRM reacts: lead stage flips Quote Sent → Signed, and the invoice engine (invoice_engine.py, gapless MLE-YYYY-NNNNNN numbers) is one click away — the Invoiced → Paid → CLIENT promotion path from the CRM PRD.

7

The rep never leaves the CRM — Documents on the lead, one-click resend

Automatic · reps stay inside the dashboard · (the file itself lives in more than one place — see below)
Lead page: Documents section
  • Every agreement version listed with a status chip — draft → sent → viewed → signed — tap to view the PDF right there (Supabase Storage signed URL)
  • Resend reopens the pre-send check pre-populated with everything confirmed last time: unchanged → old link voided, fresh link sent, same version; edited → bumps to v2, v1 auto-archived per your existing versioning rules
  • Every send/resend/view/sign lands on the lead's activity timeline (feeds the R2 timeline + audit trail)

Storage decision: Supabase Storage private bucket — same auth, RLS, signed URLs, effectively free at PDF sizes. Google Docs was considered and rejected: breaks the rep's flow, adds a second login, no row-level security.

"Never leaves the CRM" is a statement about the rep, not about the file. It means a rep never opens a second app to send, check, or resend an agreement. The signed PDF itself is deliberately in three independent places, so no single failure — including losing the CRM entirely — can take the executed document away from you.

The part that actually carries the legal weight: a signed PDF alone is weaker evidence than most people assume. What makes it hold up is the audit certificate — signer identity, email, IP, user-agent, timestamps for sent/viewed/signed, and the consent-to-electronic-signature record (ESIGN/UETA). So the rule is: the certificate is stored with the PDF, in all three copies, and the document hash is written to the documents row at execution. If a PDF ever surfaces without its matching certificate + hash, treat it as unverified. Storing only the pretty PDF is the real single-point-of-failure — not storing it only in one system.

The Contracts folder stays canonical for local filing — a small sync job (extension of organize.py's world) mirrors signed PDFs + certificates + ledger rows down from Supabase, so the (complete).pdf/archive rules keep working invisibly. Sync runs on execution and nightly; a failed sync raises rather than silently skipping, so "it's in both places" is verified, not assumed.

Nudge engine — unsigned docs chase themselves

Deterministic ladder, code-not-vibes. Runs on the hourly n8n watcher the CRM PRD already planned (Task 3.4) — agreements are just another overdue type. Numbers are defaults; Rob tunes them once real data exists.

Guardrails: max 3 customer touches per agreement · business-hours sends only · every nudge logged to the audit trail and the lead timeline · re-run never double-sends (idempotent, same rule as the overdue watcher).

Projects-tab "Contracts" link: untouched by all of this. It's the meeting demo — stays where it is for walk-in-meeting moments. The real agreement flow lives on the lead; nothing here references that tab. If it ever confuses a rep, a "demo" badge is a 5-minute fix, someday.

Legality, one line: intent (deliberate sign act) + consent (checkbox, consumer disclosures when needed) + association (audit log tied to the doc) + retention (copies emailed & stored) = binding under ESIGN/UETA (15 U.S.C. §7001).
