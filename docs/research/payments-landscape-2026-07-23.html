What you missed since ~2023 — the six big shifts

01 · Law

Stablecoins got a federal framework

GENIUS Act signed 2025-07-18 — permitted issuers, 1:1 reserves, stablecoins declared not-securities (WilmerHale, OCC rulemaking). May reduce state-by-state MTL burden for payment companies.

02 · Rails

Card networks settle in stablecoins

Visa: US USDC settlement Dec 2025 (Visa IR); Mastercard June 2026 (USDC/PYUSD/USDG/RLUSD). Practical takeaway: USDC, not BTC, is the crypto-acceptance answer now. Coinbase Commerce is dead (2026-03-31).

03 · OSS

Hyperswitch happened

Juspay open-sourced its payment switch: 43,347★, Apache-2.0, 148 processor connectors, checkout SDK, open-source PCI vault. The software layer of a payments company, free — but it never replaces the sponsor-bank relationship (FFIEC).

04 · Sales

Dual pricing is the #1 SMB pitch

Surcharging legal in ~48 states; dual pricing legal in all 50 (neutral state-by-state guide); >60% SMB adoption per industry data. Agents earn 2–3x residuals on it. And NMI bought Fee Navigator (June 2026) — AI statement audits, i.e. your PVP "show them their leaking money" play, productized.

05 · AI

Agentic commerce is a standard now

Mastercard Agent Pay, Visa Intelligent Commerce + Trusted Agent Protocol, Google AP2 (60+ partners) all landed 2025 (comparison). Chatbot-initiated payments — AIVA's lane — are becoming a first-class category, not a hack.

06 · M&A

Consolidation = your recruiting thesis

Global Payments closed $24.3B Worldpay (Jan 2026); Shift4 bought Global Blue; Nuvei acquiring Payoneer ($2.75B); Block cut ~10% incl. Square field sales (Payments Dive). Displaced reps + orphaned merchants everywhere — exactly The Network's hiring pool.
