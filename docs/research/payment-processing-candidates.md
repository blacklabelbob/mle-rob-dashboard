# The Network — Displaced Payment-Processing Sales Rep Candidates

**Prepared for:** Rob Acheson, Managing Director, My Local Everything LLC
**Prepared by:** Head of Research
**Date:** 2026-07-04
**Purpose:** PRD Phase 2.5 — identify ex-payment-processor sales reps as recruiting targets for "The Network" (local rep/node model). Thesis: payment-processing sales reps carry deep, trusted local small-business relationships ("every business needs payment processing"), and recent processor layoffs/restructurings are shaking loose talent with exactly that book of business.

**Methodology note:** 3 parallel research passes were run (company-level layoff/restructuring events; individual-level LinkedIn/news search; cross-check). All facts below are sourced with URLs and a confidence rating. Public search-engine indexing of LinkedIn's individual "open to work" posts is thin for this niche (regional field sales, not corporate HQ roles) — so named-individual yield was low despite ~50 search queries across three agents. Where individuals could not be verified, Section 3 gives ready-to-run Boolean queries so Rob can pull real names via LinkedIn Recruiter/Sales Navigator in minutes.

## 3. Ready-to-Run LinkedIn Boolean Queries

Public Google-indexed search under-surfaces individual LinkedIn "open to work" posts for field/regional sales roles (it over-indexes viral corporate-HQ layoff posts instead). These queries are built for **LinkedIn Recruiter / Sales Navigator** (or LinkedIn's own search bar, logged in) where the "open to work," "past company," and "location" filters actually work. Each is tied to a specific verified event from Section 1.

**Query 1 — Block/Square Feb 2026 layoff (highest priority):**
```
("Square" OR "Block Inc" OR "Block, Inc.") AND ("account executive" OR "territory manager" OR "relationship manager" OR "field sales" OR "merchant success" OR "restaurant sales") AND ("open to work" OR "laid off" OR "impacted")
```
Filter: Past company = Square / Block, Inc. · Location = Florida, Georgia, North Carolina (expand to national if thin) · Date posted/updated: last 6 months

**Query 2 — Global Payments/Worldpay integration:**
```
("Worldpay" OR "Global Payments") AND ("territory manager" OR "relationship manager" OR "account executive" OR "merchant services" OR "ISO relationship") AND ("open to work" OR "seeking new opportunity" OR "transition")
```
Filter: Past company = Worldpay AND/OR Global Payments · Location = FL/GA/Southeast · Note: many will still be *employed* mid-transition — pitch as "how's the Genius rebrand treating you," not just to displaced people.

**Query 3 — Heartland brand retirement:**
```
"Heartland" ("relationship manager" OR "territory manager" OR "sales" OR "merchant services") ("open to work" OR "new opportunity")
```
Filter: Past company = Heartland Payment Systems · Location = FL/GA/Southeast

**Query 4 — Fiserv / Clover:**
```
("Fiserv" OR "Clover") ("account executive" OR "channel sales" OR "ISO" OR "territory manager" OR "outside sales") ("open to work" OR "laid off" OR "seeking")
```
Filter: Past company = Fiserv · Location = FL/Southeast · Tenure filter: 3+ years (targets people with an established book, not new hires)

**Query 5 — Elavon / U.S. Bank merchant services:**
```
"Elavon" ("relationship manager" OR "territory manager" OR "sales executive") ("open to work" OR "new chapter" OR "seeking opportunities")
```
Filter: Past company = Elavon · Location = FL/Southeast · Tenure filter: 10+ years (forum reports flag long-tenured staff under pressure)

**Query 6 — Independent ISOs / merchant services resellers:**
```
("merchant services" OR "payment processing" OR "credit card processing") ("independent sales organization" OR "ISO" OR "outside sales rep" OR "territory rep") ("open to work" OR "own book of business" OR "portfolio")
```
Filter: Location = FL/Southeast · No company filter (ISOs are too fragmented to name individually) · This is the highest-signal query for people who *already* built their own local book rather than working a corporate territory — closest match to "network node" archetype.

**Query 7 — Catch-all, all six companies + Southeast geography:**
```
("Fiserv" OR "Global Payments" OR "Worldpay" OR "Elavon" OR "TSYS" OR "Heartland") AND ("sales" OR "territory" OR "relationship manager" OR "account executive") AND "open to work"
```
Filter: Location = Florida (expand ring: Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, then Atlanta, Charlotte) · Sort by "most recent"

**Execution note:** Run these in LinkedIn Recruiter/Sales Navigator (not the free public search bar) — the "open to work," past-company, and geography filters are gated behind that tier and will return dramatically better results than the public Google-indexed search this research relied on. Budget ~10 minutes per query per the task brief; Query 1 (Block/Square) and Query 6 (ISO reps) are the highest-expected-yield starting points.

## 4. Outreach Angle Notes

**Core insight: they're in sales — they answer their phone, and they read a well-targeted LinkedIn DM.** Standard cold-outreach caution (people ignore recruiters) doesn't fully apply here; treat this like a warm B2B sales sequence, not a passive job posting.

1. **Lead with their asset, not the job.** Don't pitch "we're hiring." Pitch: *"You spent [X years] building relationships with every business owner on [Main Street / in Naples]. That book of business is worth something — we want to pay you for it, not replace it."* This mirrors the PVP framing Rob already uses in GTM — show them you understand their specific value before asking for anything.

2. **Timing message differs by event:**
   - **Block/Square reps (confirmed layoff, Feb 2026):** Direct, urgent — "Saw Block's restructuring. If you're between things, let's talk."
   - **Global Payments/Worldpay reps (still employed, mid-integration):** Softer — "How's the Genius rebrand/territory consolidation treating you? Curious if your book is still fully yours." This surfaces people whose territory got diluted even if they weren't formally cut.
   - **Fiserv/Elavon/Heartland reps (soft signals — CEO churn, PIP pressure, brand retirement):** Relationship-first — "Noticed some shakeup at [company] — how are you feeling about where things are headed?" No layoff to reference, so lead with curiosity, not urgency.
   - **Independent ISO reps:** Different pitch entirely — they already own their book and likely resent corporate ISOs taking a cut. Angle: "You already built the relationships — what if you kept 100% of the value instead of splitting it with an ISO?"

3. **Call, don't just DM.** These are phone-first professionals by trade — a short, specific call ("I know you covered [territory] for [company] — got 5 minutes?") will outperform a generic LinkedIn connection request. Use the LinkedIn profile only to find a phone number/warm intro path (mutual connections, company directory, or a direct ask in the DM: "worth a quick call?").

4. **Prove you did the homework.** Reference their specific former employer's actual 2025-2026 event (from Section 1) in the first line — it signals this isn't a mass recruiting blast, consistent with the PVP "show up having already done the work" method Rob uses elsewhere.

5. **Compensation framing:** these reps are accustomed to commission-driven comp with residuals tied to a merchant portfolio. "Network node" comp should be pitched in those terms (residual/recurring, not just a flat fee) — it's the model they already understand and trust.
