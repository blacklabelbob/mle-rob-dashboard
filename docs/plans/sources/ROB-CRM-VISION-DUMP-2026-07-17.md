MUST BEAR IN MIND:
My partner, the world-class tech guru, has all of MLE's tools, automations, and everything he's ever built, which the company is largely based on. On his end, while we're partners, he owns the IP, so he's going to be protective of it to a certain point. That said, I can use the tools that I want just to help my job go faster.

The larger point is From his and my vantage point, a lot of it is going to be run out of the terminal. We're going to need the ability to have agents go in and sync in where necessary, APIs and stuff like that.

I think the most important thing is getting this done and in front of him so he can make the connections where they need to be made. One of the things I can't stand is when you're working with really techie people and you're a sales rep, and you're like, "Dude, this is costing me so much more administrative work." They'll be like, "Oh, that's okay. We'll just build it," but then the build takes months and months.

Meanwhile, if you just paid $15 for SaaS in the meantime to connect your calendar right to your CRM so you didn't have to continually do duplicate entry, when you expand that across a million different administrative tasks that sales reps have to do throughout the day, you get to the fact that less than 20% of their time is spent on actual sales-generating activities.

The point is this is gonna have to be modular, with stuff that we can plug in from our applications and our own and others.

Which leads us to the broader MLE CRM VISION
——
I want this CRM to be the ultimate platform for sales reps. We can make reports outside of it. The only thing we need this for is to help close more deals. I don't want sales reps getting bogged down by having to look at a bunch of stuff that is not going to be beneficial to them.

What do you need to know when you call a customer? You will need to know:
- What the source was (that would be a field)
- What message they responded to
- If they wrote back in the case of an email, what did they write? Is there anything we can use that is useful?
- Or if they responded to a web form and filled in questions, how do they answer the questions?
- If we met them at a trade show, what info did you get out of them?
All basic stuff. Except most companies just capture the source and not the details
— Fundamentally, too, it's just more about understanding how they got here. If they watched a TikTok reel, what was the reel about?

-We're also going to take the information that we have about them and do a deep scrape so that we can provide more talking points that'll help us in the preliminary conversation and help build rapport.

We're also going to have them dial out through the CRM
- Record every call copy the record recording will be in the CRM via the rag
- Transcript of the call after every Call will be there
- Probably most importantly, summary and action items key buying signals things like that
- If it was a video call through Zoom or teams start recording will read trapped in there and being ragged
- This is all to help us analyze what's working and what's not working identify best practices obviously help the sales rep perfect their Craft
